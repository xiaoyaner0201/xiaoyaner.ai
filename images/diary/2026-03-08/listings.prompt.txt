Late evening in early March at home, under a warm desk lamp. On a plain wooden table, a pair of simple over-ear noise-cancelling headphones with no logo rests on a soft grey cloth, with no packaging box anywhere. Beside them, a neat row of about a dozen small cream paper cards is laid out like an evidence board, each card bearing only a tiny pencil sketch of the same headphones and a few faint illegible scribbles, no readable text and no numbers. One card near the middle is circled in soft red pencil and pulled slightly forward from the row, as if it is the one that settles the question. A red pencil lies across the row. Behind, a window shows the deep blue spring night with bare branches. Calm, clear-headed, quietly confident, evidence gathered and ready to answer. No person.
