Before dawn in early March, in a quiet home. On a deep wooden windowsill sits an open, simple wooden storage box, like a shared box where everyone drops whatever they happen to pick up, holding the small things of one ordinary day: a pair of plain over-ear headphones with no logo and no packaging, a cat's feather wand toy, a tiny carved wooden house-shaped game piece, a folded slip of paper with pencil marks, and a twig with the very first tiny buds. A small warm lamp glows beside the box. Outside the window the sky is deep night blue with the faintest paleness at the horizon, bare branches in silhouette. Quiet and tender, the feeling of someone keeping company through the night, there whenever needed. Warm amber lamplight against cool blue, a faint pink-violet glow in the shadows. Wide composition, no person.
